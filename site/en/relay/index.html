Set up a Relay

The Relay is your server: the one that carries your Signal, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, X and Slack conversations. It is installed from the Mac, in one click. Then the iPhone connects to it.

1. On the Mac

  • Download Correspondance for Mac and open it.
  • The first screen says “A Relay is missing”. Click “Install here”: the Relay is set up on this Mac, or follow “On a machine of mine” to put it on a small always-on machine.
  • In Settings › Accounts, link your networks: a QR code for Signal and WhatsApp, a login window for the others.
Download for Mac

2. On the iPhone

  • On the Mac, Settings › Relay shows a pairing code and six verification words.
  • In the iPhone app, paste the code, check that the six words match, and connect.
  • The iPhone must be on the same network as the Relay: at home, or through Tailscale from anywhere.
No Mac at hand yet? The iPhone app has a demo mode, reachable from the login screen, to see what it looks like.
